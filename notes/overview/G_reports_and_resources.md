### 1.1 `notes/gradient_audit.tex` (+ .pdf) — "Is the auxiliary loss doing anything?"

- **Date:** 23 September 2026.
- **Purpose:** theory notes for deciding whether an auxiliary loss L_k in L_0 + λL_k shaped training, plus the measurements of `tools/gradient_audit.py`. Sections: why loss values carry no information; one step of GD; equilibrium/linear response; drift vs minibatch noise; Adam geometry; what uncertainty weighting, GradNorm, MGDA/PCGrad and black-box search optimise; fair-comparison protocol; per-loss case studies; how to read the audit; exercises; measurements.
- **Key theory claims:**
  - Comparing ‖∇L_0‖ with λ‖∇L_k‖ is meaningful only far from convergence; at a converged checkpoint the MSE per-batch norm is mostly noise.
  - Fair comparison: measure parity λ* at initialisation, train a log-bracket around it, evaluate at fixed budget on the last checkpoint, check signatures (own-loss gain ∝ λ, base-loss cost ∝ λ², share → 1 and cosine → −1 at the trained checkpoint). This is DECISIONS §16 with parity measured at init.
  - Minkowski: soft mask s_p(u)=σ((ŷ_p−u)/τ_u), τ_u=max(0.1u, τ_min); pixels in the gradient band N_u ∝ u f(u), so for heavy tails thresholds 31–150 mm/h see almost no pixels (explains "83% of gradient from u ≤ 0.28 mm/h"); widening τ (anneal) cannot change the share.
  - SSIM bug: dry-on-dry windows score SSIM≈0 because c1c2 ≤ 9e-8 R^4 ≪ ε=1e-6, so the loss floor is 0.465 ⇒ ~93% of windows dry; fixes: fixed data range with ε ≪ c1c2, or mean over windows wet in either field.
  - Spectral is translation-invariant (cannot penalise displacement); wet area is the single-threshold (u=0.1, τ=0.05) Minkowski area case, blind to intensity; optical flow's teacher is the smooth coarse input.
- **Measurements (N=512 batches × 128 patches, K=8 jackknife, anneal 0.1, fp32, GPU 1; raw output `eval_results/gradient_audit/2026-09-23/`):**
  - Parity at init: Minkowski λ=1e-4, λ*=1.05e-3, r=0.095, cos +0.78; spectral 5e-4 / 0.298 / 1.7e-3 / +0.96; SSIM 1.6e-4 / 0.932 / 1.7e-4 / −0.77; wet area 2e-2 / 2.00e-2 / 1.0 / +0.85 (clips 64% of initial batches); optical flow 4e-5 / 1.09 / 3.7e-5 / +0.79.
  - Vanilla checkpoint: MSE noise fraction 0.96 (per-batch norm 3.0e-4 vs signal 6.1e-5); the first audit's "70× below parity" is ≈18× after noise correction and ≈600× at init.
  - Equilibrium test at own checkpoint: Minkowski r=1.22, cos −0.986, Adam share 1.10 (active); wet area 0.74 / −0.967 / 1.03 (active); SSIM 0.157 / +0.470 / 0.038 (bystander); spectral 0.021 / +0.915 / 0.022 (bystander); optical flow 0.0066 / −0.474 / 0.008 (inert).
  - Own-loss test vs vanilla: Minkowski −64.8% [−65.9, −63.8]; wet area −13.9%; optical flow −2.6% [−6.4, +1.2]; spectral +2.5%; SSIM +0.1%. Spectral's wrong sign is explained by under-training at 25 epochs.
- **Conclusions:** spectral, SSIM, optical flow were bystanders; Minkowski and wet area active. Use init λ* as the unit (Study-1 weights were ~1e-1, 1e-3, 1e-4, 4e-5, 1 × parity for Minkowski, spectral, SSIM, optical flow, wet area). Brackets should be placed so the equilibrium test holds at end of training, not copied from Minkowski's working point. The note flags its ML references as from memory.

To be redone with the quantile form (bracket queued, §41).
- **Gradient budget (Adam scale invariance; `scripts/evaluate/grad_norm_terms.py`, `logs/grad_norm_terms_v4adv.txt`):** energy original form: reward norm 8.3e-4, anchor 1.2e-4, retention 0.65, reward share 0.13%, cos −0.01; dist original: 8.3e-4 / 0.7e-4 / 0 / 99% / +0.99; energy quantile_mass: 5.0e-4, share 0.07%; dist quantile_mass: share 96%. The energy fine-tune is "more FM training at low LR"; parity needs w_r ×~1000.
- **Fine-tune results (DECISIONS §38; 16 members, heun-16, `fm_mink_latest.pth`; clean FM evaluated twice for noise):** heavy subset (1,024 patches): exceedances of 1,537 observed: FM 458/393, FM+dist 580, FM+energy 411; RL bias FM −30/−36/−44 and −33/−36/−36, dist −23/−20/−15, energy −31/−33/−33; FSS 31.7 mm 21 px 0.707/0.707, 0.741, 0.726; RAPSD distance 1.14/1.19, 0.81, 1.24; CRPS 0.1613/0.1614, 0.1598, 0.1615. Random subset (4,096) exceedance ratio 0.272, 0.517, 0.228. FM+dist helps beyond eval noise but its reward barely moved (0.351→0.339); FM+energy sits inside clean-FM spread (reward 0.199→0.205); its GPD shape error 0.011 is best in the table next to a weak tail.
- **Why the original reward misses heavy rain:** kernel width τ_u = 0.005u; a 17 mm core under a 20.2 mm threshold gets ∂s/∂f ≈ 1.7e-13; at 50.1 mm ≈ 5e-58 (underflows in fp32) — the "reach" problem. Summed over pixels a threshold's pull → N p(u) regardless of τ (pixel-mass limit). Measured on clean hourly FM: 73% of |∂R/∂z| on pixels < 1 mm (heavy subset), 89% (random); 3.7%/0.2% on ≥ 20 mm.
- **Fix, decided by researcher 2026-10-29:** `REWARD_FORM: quantile_mass` = quantile (horizontal/rank) form of the area channel, q_j = log(1+f_(n_j)), plus inverse-mass weights (n_j+1)^−α, α=1, for perimeter and Euler; original stays as `REWARD_FORM: threshold` (default). Properness kept (weights depend only on the target). Checks: `tests/test_reward_form.py` 13 pass; share on ≥ 20 mm pixels 3.7% → 40.9%, < 1 mm 73% → 16% (heavy); coupling check distributional −31.6% (orig −28.1%), energy −35.1% (orig −32.5%); reward sampling noise 11.4% → 18.9% of mean.
- **Where the FM loss looks (`fm_loss_intensity_share.py`, `logs/fm_loss_intensity_share_v4adv.txt`):** heavy: pixels <1/1–10/10–20/20–50/≥50 mm = 92.3/7.2/0.44/0.12/0.009%; |∂L/∂v| 69.9/27.3/2.1/0.66/0.06%; projection share +327/−170/−37/−17/−2.5%. Pixels ≥ 20 mm carry 0.72% (heavy) and 0.10% (random). Every bin ≥ 10 mm has negative projection share: where bulk and tail conflict, the bulk wins. Withdrawn claim (2026-10-29): "any pixel-averaged loss weights a level by its pixel mass". Reweighting the FM loss tilts the optimum (weighted mean), which is why a proper feature-space reward is preferred.
- **"Stability in the tails":** first-order argument shows rank and count readings have the same SNR (withdrawn claim: rank "~6× more stable"). Synthetic test (`tail_reading_synthetic.py`) and real ensemble (`tail_reading_snr.py`, `logs/tail_reading_snr_v4adv.txt`): ensemble sits 1–3 spreads below target above ~5 mm; rank reading only 1.1–1.5× better; 55% of members never reach 20 mm, 73% never reach 50 mm. Case for quantile_mass rests on reach and gradient share.
- **General hypothesis:** pixel-averaged objectives under-predict heavy rain mainly because of gradient allocation (~1%); an auxiliary objective helps if (i) larger tail gradient share, (ii) reach, (iii) proper score. Candidate competitors: threshold-weighted CRPS, pinball, soft FSS, GPD likelihood, inverse-mass-weighted FM loss.
- **Plan (2026-10-30):** (1) FM + dist reward with quantile form, after fixing the proximal anchor to the scaled residual (`config_v4_adv_fm_dist_qm.yaml`); (2) FM + energy quantile form with w_r rebalanced ×~1000 plus a w_r = 0 control; (3) port quantile_mass to the U-Net loss with tests and a new bracket.
