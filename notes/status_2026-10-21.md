## 5. Caveats to keep in view

- The corrected climatology applies pixel repairs and radar exclusions, not tile-hour rejections,
  so it overstates what reaches the dataset (the worst tile-hours are rejected whole).
- The Romanian radars stay at 4-5x the median radar after correction; partly climate (no jump at
  any boundary), partly intermittent artefacts. Stated as a limitation.
- Pre-break fields are 1.3-6.5x more fragmented at 0.1 mm; they stay in training until the ablation.
